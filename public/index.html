Participation terms

Terms & Conditions

Campaign scope

This two-week Green Freedom 80 campaign collects one participation record per mobile number for a Young Indians climate-awareness initiative in Madurai.

What your pledge means

Your submission creates one digital tree in this website’s community forest. It does not independently verify or guarantee a physical tree planting outcome.

Participation rule

Only one accepted submission is allowed per normalized Indian mobile number. A duplicate mobile number will be rejected.

Accuracy

Please enter your real name, mobile number, locality, and address details for campaign administration. Do not submit confidential or third-party data without permission.

Data handling

Privacy Policy

What we store

Young Indians stores your name, mobile number, locality, and address details for this campaign. Public views show only aggregate counts and privacy-safe tree positions.

Who can view the Sheet

The campaign uses a Google Sheet chosen by the campaign owner. Anyone who obtains that Sheet link can view submitted details.

What is not shown publicly

Your name, mobile number, address details, certificate identifiers, and exact submission timestamps are not exposed through the public website APIs.

Digital tree map

Tree coordinates are privacy-safe locality-level visual points. They are not your home address or exact personal location.
