Data handling

Privacy Policy

Young Indians stores participant details for this campaign. Public views show only aggregate counts and privacy-safe tree positions.

What we store

Young Indians stores your name, mobile number, locality, and address details for this campaign and sapling-delivery administration.

Who can view the Sheet

The campaign uses a Google Sheet chosen by the campaign owner. Anyone who obtains that Sheet link can view submitted details.

What is not shown publicly

Your name, mobile number, address details, certificate identifiers, and exact submission timestamps are not exposed through the public website APIs.

Digital tree map

Hotspots on the public map are privacy-safe locality-level visual points. They are not your home address or exact personal location.

Public views

Public views show only aggregate counts and privacy-safe tree positions so the city can see momentum without exposing personal information.

Participant care

Please avoid sharing confidential information through this form. Only submit the details needed for campaign administration and tree-sapling delivery coordination.
