Participation terms

Terms & Conditions

This two-week Green Freedom 80 campaign collects one participation record per mobile number for a Young Indians climate-awareness initiative in Madurai.

Campaign scope

The campaign runs as a two-week public participation drive around India’s 80th Independence Day and is intended to encourage climate action across Madurai.

What your pledge means

Your submission creates one digital tree on the website’s locality-level participation map. Young Indians will deliver a tree sapling to your home after your digital pledge, and you are expected to nurture and care for that sapling.

Participation rule

Only one accepted submission is allowed per normalized Indian mobile number. Duplicate mobile numbers will be rejected.

Accuracy and responsibility

Please enter your real name, mobile number, locality, and address details for campaign administration and sapling delivery coordination. Do not submit confidential or third-party data without permission.

Delivery intent

Sapling delivery is part of the campaign intent described on this site. Participants are expected to plant, protect, water, and nurture the delivered sapling.

Public experience

The public site shows only aggregate counts and privacy-safe locality-level visual markers. It does not publish home addresses or exact personal locations.
